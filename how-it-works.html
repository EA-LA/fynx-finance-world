How it works

One clear system for money spread across trading accounts.

Fynx is being designed to combine cash, broker balances, funded-account limits, and tax planning in one controlled financial workspace.

01

Connect or add your accounts

Add supported bank and broker connections, then enter funded accounts that need rule tracking. Connections will only be activated after the required security and provider integrations are ready.

02

See your real available capital

Fynx separates money currently deployed in the market, available dry powder, and funds reserved for taxes so the total is easier to understand.

03

Track funded-account rules

Monitor profit targets, daily loss limits, maximum drawdown, evaluation status, and payout eligibility from a single view.

04

Build safer money routines

Set alerts and planning rules for withdrawals, tax reserves, and risk limits. Fynx does not provide investment, tax, or legal advice.
