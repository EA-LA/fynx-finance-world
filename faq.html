Common questions.

Clear answers about the FYNX private beta, account connections, product data, and waitlist.

Is FYNX available now?

FYNX is currently preparing for private beta. Join the waitlist to receive launch and early-access updates.

Does joining the waitlist require payment?

No. Joining the waitlist is free and does not require a card.

Is the dashboard showing live money?

Public product-preview screens may use simulated data to demonstrate the planned experience.

Will FYNX connect to banks and brokers?

FYNX is being designed around supported connections plus manually entered accounts. Connection availability may vary by provider.

Does FYNX provide investment or tax advice?

No. FYNX is an organization and information product, not a bank, broker-dealer, investment adviser, tax adviser, or law firm.

How will my information be used?

Waitlist information is used for FYNX early-access and product-launch communication. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How can I contact FYNX?
